The Three Architectural Proposals

Designed to address different levels of curatorial ambition, operational control, and exhibition preparedness.

Proposal 1

Digital Monograph & Aesthetic Showcase

A pure, high-performance visual exhibition website focused on the aesthetic legacy of Lee An-cheng. Zero backoffice, no database, and $0/mo hosting maintenance.

Static SSG Zero Backoffice Bilingual ZH/EN $0/mo Hosting
  • Atmospheric Scroll Theme: Light-to-dark illumination transition tied to scroll depth (James Turrell inspired).
  • Horizontal Handscroll Gallery: Gesture-driven unrolling simulation for horizontal works (*手卷 / 橫披*).
  • Curated Narrative Rooms: Static viewing rooms combining painting views, archival photos, and curator essays (David Zwirner style).
  • High-Res Deep Zoom: Client-side zoom on Xuan paper fibers, ink bleeding, and brushstrokes.
  • 1959–2015 Chronology: Interactive biographical timeline & institutional collections roster.
  • No Backoffice CMS (Layout updates require code edits)
  • No Custodian CRM or Location Tracking
Timeline: 3–4 Weeks
Hosting: $0 / mo

Proposal 2

Dynamic Exhibition Portal & Showcase CMS

An interactive public frontend backed by a bilingual Admin Portal (CMS) that enables family editors to reorder featured works, curate digital rooms, and publish essays.

Dynamic Frontend Showcase Admin CMS IIIF Deep Zoom Editorial Engine
  • All Features from Proposal 1 (Atmospheric scroll, handscroll gallery, deep zoom).
  • Showcase Layout Builder: Reorder featured paintings, toggle public visibility, and customize exhibition titles.
  • Multi-Axis Archive Filter: Dynamic search across year slider, ink medium, mounting, and period (Olafur Eliasson style).
  • Editorial & News CMS: Publish bilingual essays, press releases, exhibition news, and press kit downloads.
  • WebGL Ink-Bleed Transitions: Fluid ripple effects on hover and page navigation (TeamLab inspired).
  • No 3-Custodian Physical Logistics Tracker
  • No Automated France/USA Museum Export Crosswalks
Timeline: 8–10 Weeks
Hosting: ~$30–$60 / mo

Proposal 3

Full Museum-Grade Curating & Art CRM Suite

The ultimate institutional solution: 52-Piece Inventory TMS, Epigraphy & Seal Matrix, 3-Custodian Logistics CRM, Automated France/USA/EU Export Engine, and 3D Virtual Gallery.

Museum TMS 3-Custodian CRM Export Engine (Joconde/CDWA) 3D Virtual Gallery Digital COA
  • All Features from Proposals 1 & 2 (Complete frontend showcase & editorial CMS).
  • 52-Piece Inventory TMS & Epigraphy: Dual calendar (Gregorian/Minguo/Lunar), Seal Matrix (*朱文/白文*) bounding box tool, and inscription transcriptions.
  • 3-Custodian Private Logistics CRM: Track exact custody across the 3 family owners, location status, packing lists, and loan agreements for shipments abroad.
  • Automated International Crosswalk Engine: 1-click export to France (POP Base Joconde), USA (CDWA/VRA Core), and Europe (LIDO 1.1).
  • 3D Virtual Gallery & Spatial Curator: Real-scale millimeter wall placement, lighting studio (3000K–5000K), and spatial audio walkthroughs.
  • VIP Viewing Rooms & Digital COA: Tokenized collector links, condition logs, and verifiable digital certificates of authenticity.
Timeline: 16–20 Weeks
Hosting: ~$120–$250 / mo
